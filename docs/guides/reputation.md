# Reputation

Reputation automates moderation and rewards constructive participation.

## Reputation change rules

| Condition | Change |
|---|---:|
| Your question was upvoted | +10 |
| Your answer was upvoted | +10 |
| Your answer was accepted | +15 |
| You accepted an answer | +2 |
| Your suggested edit was accepted | +2 |
| You downvoted an answer | -1 |
| Your question was downvoted | -2 |
| Your answer was downvoted | -2 |

## Additional rules

- New accounts start with `0` reputation; it becomes `1` after account activation.
- Reputation cannot fall below `1`; once at `1`, further decreases that would take it below `1` are ignored.
- Users can earn a maximum of `200` reputation per UTC day.
- If reputation gains would push a user over `200` in a day, subsequent gains that day are ignored.
- Reputation from accepted answers is exempt from the `200` daily cap.
- No reputation is awarded for accepting your own answer.
